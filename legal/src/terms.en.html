2. The service

aljana calculates prayer times, the Qibla, the sky, the Moon, the Hilal and eclipses, and gives access to the Muṣḥaf and to texts of remembrance. Part of the app is free, with no time limit, including the complete Qur’an and today’s prayer times. The other features are reserved for aljana Premium; they are marked in the app.

aljana is a calculation and reading tool. Prayer times, crescent forecasts and eclipses are astronomical calculations; aljana issues no religious rulings and does not announce the start of months. The opinions quoted come from sources identified in the app. For any religious decision, refer to the scholars and bodies of your community.
