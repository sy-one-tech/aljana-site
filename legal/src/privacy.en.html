Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This English version is a translation provided for convenience. If it differs from the French version, the French version prevails.

aljana calculates your prayer times, the Qibla, the sky, the Moon, the Hilal and eclipses directly on your device. This page states plainly the information that leaves it, where it goes, why, and for how long.

1. In short

  • No account, no sign-up: aljana does not ask for your name, email address or phone number.
  • Premium purchases go through the App Store or Google Play: aljana never sees your payment details or your identity (see “Premium purchases”).
  • No advertising, no trackers, no analytics tools, no crash reports sent.
  • aljana’s servers do not record your location, your settings or how you use the app.
  • aljana does not sell your data or use it to track you across other apps or websites.

2. Your location

How it is obtained

Your device’s location is read only when you tap the location button, and only if you allow it (“while using the app” permission). You can also choose a place without ever sharing your location: search for a city, pick a suggested city, or enter coordinates by hand.

What it is used for

Prayer times, the Qibla, the sky, the Moon, the Hilal and eclipses are calculated on your device from the chosen place. The current place and your last five places are stored on your device so you are not asked again.

What is sent, and to whom

Some information cannot be calculated offline. To obtain it, the app sends the coordinates of the chosen place to specialised services:

  • Open-Meteo: time zone and altitude of the place, weather and cloud cover (Sky, Hilal and Eclipses screens), horizon relief around the place (Hilal screen).
  • BigDataCloud: the name of the city matching your location, only after you tap the location button.

When you search for a city, the text you type is sent to Open-Meteo (place search) and, if nothing is found, to Photon (Komoot’s geographic search service, based on OpenStreetMap).

aljana sends these services no name, no identifier and no information about your device. As with any Internet connection, they also receive your IP address. They process this data under their own privacy policies.

3. Downloaded content

To display the Muṣḥaf, the translations of its meanings and the tafsīr, the app downloads pages, fonts and texts:

  • from aljana’s servers, hosted by Cloudflare (Muṣḥaf pages, translations, tafsīr);
  • from Quran Foundation (page fonts of the Ḥafṣ Muṣḥaf).

These requests contain only the page number, verse or language requested. Your settings, location and reading are not transmitted. aljana’s servers use your IP address briefly to limit abuse (number of requests per minute); aljana keeps no log of it. Our hosting provider Cloudflare processes the technical connection data needed to route requests and protect the service, under its own policy.

4. What stays on your device

The following are stored only on your device and are never sent to aljana:

  • your settings (calculation method, ʿaṣr school, adhan, reminders, language, display, accessibility);
  • the chosen place and your recent places;
  • your page, bookmarks and night mode in the Muṣḥaf;
  • your tasbīḥ counter;
  • your Hilal observations and their notes;
  • downloaded pages, fonts, translations and tafsīr texts;
  • the status of your Premium access, kept so that Premium works offline.

You delete them by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it. Depending on your device settings, they may be included in its automatic backup (iCloud or Google backup), managed by the maker of your device’s operating system.

5. Notifications, adhan and compass

  • Reminders and the adhan are scheduled on your device, from times calculated on your device. aljana does not use server-sent notifications and collects no notification identifier.
  • Permission to send notifications or alarms is requested only if you turn on the adhan or a reminder.
  • The Qibla compass reads your device’s orientation sensors, with your consent. These readings stay on your device.

6. Premium purchases

Premium purchases go through Apple’s App Store or Google Play, with your store’s payment methods. Your store processes the payment: aljana never receives your payment details, your name or your account’s email address. Apple and Google then act under their own privacy policies.

To verify your purchases and unlock Premium on your devices, aljana uses RevenueCat, a provider acting on our behalf. The app sends it:

  • a random identifier created by the app; it contains neither your name nor your email address, but it links your purchases together, so it is pseudonymous data;
  • the purchase information provided by your store (offer, date, price, currency, store country, renewal, refund, transaction number);
  • technical information (device system and version, app version, language, IP address).

Apple and Google also inform RevenueCat directly of renewals, cancellations and refunds. For the 1-month pass on iPhone, aljana’s server reads the purchase history from RevenueCat, using this identifier, to calculate when your access ends; it keeps nothing.

This information is used only to provide Premium, restore your purchases, prevent fraud and follow aljana’s sales as a whole. It is never used for advertising or to track you. RevenueCat processes it under its privacy policy.

7. Sharing and external links

Nothing is shared without your action. When you tap “Share” (Qibla, eclipse, Hilal observation, verse, supplication, hadith), you see the content and choose the recipient yourself. Depending on the screen, this content may include your city name or its coordinates.

Some links open third-party websites (for example Quran Foundation, QuranEnc, HadeethEnc, or Google Calendar to add an eclipse to your calendar). They open only if you tap them, and their own privacy policies then apply.

8. The aljana.app website

  • City shown: the city shown on the website is derived from your IP address by our hosting provider, Cloudflare, at city level. It is neither stored nor shared. The website never asks for your device’s location.
  • Fonts: the website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address.
  • Browser storage: the language you choose is kept by your browser; the launch screen and scroll position are kept only for the duration of your visit.
  • No cookies, no analytics. Cloudflare processes the technical connection data needed to serve the pages and protect the website.

9. Retention and security

  • Data on your device: until you delete it or uninstall the app.
  • Coordinates and searches sent to the services in section 2: aljana keeps no copy; each service keeps them under its own policy.
  • IP address on aljana’s servers: for the time needed to handle the request; aljana keeps no log.
  • Purchase history at RevenueCat: as long as it is useful to restore your purchases and handle a claim, unless you ask us to delete it (see “Your rights”).
  • SY-ONE’s accounting records: they are drawn from Apple’s and Google’s sales reports, without your identity, and kept for ten years as required by the French Commercial Code.
  • Messages you send us: for the time needed to answer and follow up on your request.

All connections made by the app and the website are encrypted (HTTPS).

10. Legal bases and transfers outside Europe

Why we may process this data

  • Providing the service you ask for (calculations for the chosen place, downloads, Premium purchases and access): performance of the terms of use you accept.
  • Reading your device’s location: your consent, given through the location permission, which you can withdraw at any time in your device settings.
  • Protecting the service against abuse and fraud (request limits, purchase verification) and following sales as a whole: SY-ONE’s legitimate interest.
  • Keeping accounting records: legal obligation.

Transfers outside the European Union

Some providers are based or process data outside the European Union, including RevenueCat, Cloudflare and Google for the website fonts (United States), BigDataCloud (Australia) and Quran Foundation. Depending on the provider, these transfers rely on the safeguards provided by the GDPR (an adequacy decision, including the EU and US Data Privacy Framework for certified companies, or the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses) or, for a service you request yourself, on their necessity to provide it to you.

11. Your rights

Under applicable law, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, and the right to withdraw your consent at any time.

  • Data on your device: you keep direct control of it and can delete it at any time.
  • Purchase data: since aljana has no accounts, write to us with the order number from your receipt (Apple or Google). It is enough for us to find your history at RevenueCat, send it to you or delete it. We will not ask for more information than needed. Deletion does not cancel an active subscription, which you cancel in your store, and does not affect the accounting records the law requires us to keep.

We answer within one month. You may also lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country (in France, the CNIL).

12. Children

aljana can be used by everyone. It asks no one for personal data, including children. The only data linked to a user is the purchase data described above; purchases are subject to your store’s parental controls.

13. Changes

If a change to aljana alters how your data is handled (for example an account or a new service), this policy will be updated before that change becomes available, and the date at the top of this page will be revised.
